What Coordinators Value

The Things That Actually Matter When You're Running an Event.

01

You set the tone — I follow your lead.

Every event has its own flow and you know it better than anyone. Tell me how you like to run things, and that's how I'll operate. I'm not here to freelance around your plan — I'm here to execute within it.

02

I communicate proactively — you shouldn't have to follow up.

Timeline questions, tech riders, venue logistics — I reach out before you have to. If something changes on my end, you'll hear from me first. I aim to be the vendor you never have to worry about.

03

I respect the run-of-show, full stop.

I read cue sheets, hit start and stop times, and coordinate directly with your AV team. The timeline you built is the timeline I follow. No surprises that circle back to you.

04

I take care of your clients — and they'll know you sent them to me.

I take time before every event to understand what matters to your client. They feel heard and well taken care of. That experience reflects on you for recommending me — and that's exactly how it should work.

05

I adapt without making it your problem.

Ceremony running late? Last-minute song request? I've been performing in unpredictable environments for two decades. I handle it quietly and move on. You'll find out how it resolved, not that it happened.

06

One contact for the whole music picture.

Need a full band for a larger event? Through my Austin musician network, I can coordinate that too. You make one referral — I handle everything from there.
