Why Choose Me for Your Wedding

A Wedding Isn't Just an Event. It's a Sequence of Unrepeatable Moments.

The best wedding musicians don't just play songs — they shepherd emotion. They know the difference between the hush before a bride walks in, the electricity of a first dance, and the release of a packed reception floor at 10 PM.

I've spent over a decade learning exactly how to read those moments. I adjust my tempo, my genre, even my lyrics in real time — without a set list cue, without being asked, because I'm genuinely present and paying attention to your room.

This isn't something you can fake. It comes from playing thousands of events across every conceivable environment — and making the deliberate choice to specialize in weddings because the stakes, and the reward, are unlike anything else in live performance.

Coverage

From the First Note to the Last Dance.

I can cover every musical moment of your wedding day — each one treated with its own intentional approach.

Cocktail Hour

Elegant, ambient, and social. The cocktail hour sets the tone for the reception. I keep the music sophisticated and present without overwhelming conversation — the perfect backdrop for guests mingling and photos being taken.

Reception & Dancing

When it's time to celebrate, I'm as comfortable getting a crowd on their feet as I am setting an intimate mood. My layered, full-band sound means the energy stays high without dead air between songs.

First Dance & Dinner

Your first dance is sacred — I'll have your song down cold, and I know how to wrap the room in silence right before it begins. During dinner, I read the room: soft and romantic when the mood calls for it, building energy as the evening progresses.

Ceremony

Available on request. The processional, the ceremony music, the recessional — each piece chosen to honor the weight of the moment. Whether you want a traditional classical arrangement or a contemporary acoustic version of your favorite song, I'll nail it.

Special Requests

Have a song that isn't in the catalog? I'll learn it. Seriously — just request it during your consultation and I'll have it performance-ready for your day. This is one of the things clients mention most in testimonials.

Full Band Option

Want more scale for your reception? I can coordinate a complete live band through my Austin network. Same trusted communication, bigger production. Available at a premium — ask during your consultation.
