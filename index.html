Why I Am Different

Why the Music Choice Matters More Than Most People Realize

Hiring someone who can play songs is easy. Finding someone who understands what this night means — and carries that weight for every moment, for every person in your room — that's what's actually worth looking for.

Reads the Room

From high-energy cocktail hour to a quiet, emotional first dance, I know when to lift the energy and when to let silence speak. I adjust tempo, lyrics, and style in real time — without being asked.

10+ Years of Experience

From New York jazz clubs to stages in Puerto Rico, Spain, Sweden and London — performing across wildly different venues and cultures gave me a range and adaptability that shows up in every event I play today.

Endless Repertoire

Hundreds of songs across Pop, Jazz, Latin, R&B, Country, Classical, and more. Can't find your song on the list? I'll learn it. Seriously — just ask during your consultation.

A Complete One-Man Show

Piano, vocals, guitar, live looping, and a backing drum kit — all seamlessly layered by one performer. Clients consistently say it sounds like a full band. Because it does.

Vendor-Fluent

With years of experience in weddings, private events, and working with corporations like BetMGM, SandsRx, and many more, I speak the language of event and wedding planners alike. Let's make this process seamless for you.

Full-Band Coordination

Need more than a solo act? Through my deep Austin music network, I can coordinate a full live band for your event — with the same attention to detail you'd expect from me directly.

The Process

Simple. Thoughtful. Stress-Free.

Every great event starts with a great conversation. Here's how I make the whole process easy on you.

1

Free Consultation

Fill out the short form below and I'll reach out to schedule a free call. We'll talk through your event, your vision, and anything that matters to you — no pressure, just a conversation.

2

Tailor Your Set List

Want to hand-pick every song? We'll build it together. Rather hand me the keys and let me handle it? Even better — just tell me the vibe and I'll take care of the rest. Either way, your must-play song gets played.

3

Enjoy Your Event

I arrive early, set up quietly, and deliver a performance calibrated to exactly what your event needs. You enjoy your moment. I handle the rest.
