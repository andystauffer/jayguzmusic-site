Why Choose Me for Corporate

I Understand Corporate Because I've Lived It.

Most musicians understand music. I also understand corporate environments — because I've spent years navigating them professionally alongside my performance career.

As an Account Executive at The Knot, a leading wedding and events platform, I work daily with event professionals, understand vendor dynamics, and know that a corporate event isn't just a party — it's a reflection of your brand, your culture, and your leadership.

I'm polished during executive dinners, energizing at holiday parties, and seamlessly professional when C-suite leadership is in the room. I will never be the problem at your event.

Brand-calibrated performance

I match my energy, volume, and style to your company's culture — not my own preferences.

Crowd engagement when you want it

I can warm up a room of strangers at a networking event or fade elegantly into background during a formal dinner.

Reliable on the run-of-show

I work off your event timeline, hit cues, and coordinate seamlessly with your event planner or AV team.

Ideal For

Any Corporate Occasion, Handled With Precision.

Annual Galas

Make your signature event unforgettable. I elevate black-tie affairs with sophisticated live entertainment that complements the occasion without competing with it.

Holiday Parties

From festive and fun to tastefully professional, I set exactly the tone your team celebration deserves — inclusive, energizing, and memorable for all the right reasons.

Client Entertainment

Impress your most important clients with an evening they'll remember and talk about. Live piano creates an atmosphere that signals taste, investment, and attention to detail.

Product Launches

The right soundtrack makes a product launch feel like a cultural moment. I can mirror your brand energy — from understated elegance to high-energy celebration.

Networking Events

Live music dissolves the awkwardness of a room full of strangers. My ambient-but-present style keeps energy flowing and conversations starting, without overpowering the room.

Conferences & Dinners

Pre-conference cocktail receptions, awards dinners, closing night celebrations — I have the versatility to serve every moment across a multi-day corporate event.

Live, Recorded, or Both — Your Call

If your event calls for a specific artist or recording, I'll tell you straight — sometimes the original is the right choice, and there's no shame in that. For the final portion of the evening, many companies love transitioning to a curated background playlist. Since my sound system is already in place, we can build this in at a fraction of what a separate DJ would cost. Same audio quality, no extra logistics, no second vendor to manage.
