About Jay

Bringing the vibe.

New York, Austin, Puerto Rico, Spain, Sweden, Mexico, London — a lot of rooms, a lot of crowds, and one thing that never changes: the vibe is everything.

Bringing the Vibe

Hey there!

My name's Jay, and I'd love to play your next event. I'm primarily a pop piano/singing act, with a bit of guitar. I play a wide variety of genres, and I'm also happy to learn new songs at your request — so don't be shy!

I've played events everywhere from New York to Austin, Texas, Puerto Rico, Spain, Sweden, Mexico, London and many more. I believe that the vibe is everything, so whether you're looking for a mellower musician for a low-key event or you want a full-on party, I'm your man!

My catalog is well over a few hundred songs, and I do top 40 contemporary, singer/songwriter, pop staples and older classics. A few examples of artists I play: Elton John, Billy Joel, Alicia Keys, Maroon 5, Harry Styles, Cyndi Lauper, Fleetwood Mac, the Lumineers, and much, much more.

Thanks for considering me for your next event — I look forward to bringing the jams for you and your guests!

The Arsenal

One Performer. A Full Sound.

My setup is engineered to sound like a full band — because it basically is. Here's what I bring to every show.

Piano & Keys

The foundation of everything. My piano playing anchors the entire performance — from classical ceremony pieces to jazz standards to pop anthems. Classically trained, endlessly versatile.

Vocals

Smooth, expressive, and perfectly calibrated to the moment. My voice is as flexible as my piano — equally at home on a heartfelt ballad or a high-energy crowd favorite.

Guitar

Acoustic and electric. The guitar adds texture, warmth, and range that the keys alone can't provide — perfect for intimate moments or adding a different sonic color to the room.

Light Percussion

The rhythmic backbone that gives every song its full-band feel. Subtle enough for a dinner setting, powerful enough for a reception — layered in live alongside everything else.

Full Band (Upgrade)

Need the full production? Through my Austin network, I can coordinate a complete live band — horn section, strings, rhythm section, the works. Contact for custom pricing.
