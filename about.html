About Jason

Twenty Years In.
Still the Best Part of My Day Is Getting on Stage.

From New York clubs to Caribbean cruise ships to Austin's most meaningful events — the path has been long and winding. The reason for it has always been the same.

The Journey

A Long Road, a Lot of Stages, and One Thread That Never Changed.

I'm Jason — Jay to most people who've known me a while. I've been performing professionally for over twenty years, and the path here wasn't exactly a straight line.

I started with classical and jazz training, then found my way to New York City — where the expectations are high and nobody's obligated to pay attention. That environment sharpened my instincts fast. From there, I contracted with Caribbean cruise lines as a traveling musician, performing night after night for international audiences on ships that literally moved. And from there, time in San Juan, Puerto Rico, where Latin music got into my blood and permanently changed how I hear and play everything.

Along the way, I explored. I wrote and released some of my own music. I played with production, dabbled in different styles — R&B, country, indie, Latin, classical. Not out of restlessness, but because music just pulls you toward the next thing if you're genuinely curious about it. I tried a lot. I learned from all of it.

But through all of it, what I kept coming back to was simple: the feeling of performing live, for people who are fully present in the room. That has never gotten old. That's still what gets me out of bed.

Austin was inevitable. I came about five years ago, fell in love with the scene immediately — the warmth, the genuine audiences, the city's deep respect for live music — left for a while when life required it, and came back for good. This is home. These are the events I want to be part of.

Two Careers, One Purpose

Where Music Meets the Real World.

Somewhere along the way — gradually, not dramatically — my career moved toward events. Weddings, corporate gatherings, private parties. I still love Austin's live music scene as much as ever. But there's something about performing for people who are fully invested in the evening that I keep gravitating toward.

At a bar, you're playing for whoever walks in. At a wedding or a corporate gala, everyone in that room showed up for a reason. The music isn't background noise — it's part of why they'll remember the night. That kind of intentionality is where I find performing most meaningful.

My professional life happened to move in the same direction. I'm currently an Account Executive at The Knot — one of the leading wedding and events platforms — which means I understand the event world from the inside. I speak the language of event coordinators. I understand timelines, vendor protocols, and the real pressure of making someone's most important day go exactly right. That context makes everything easier for the people I work with.

The Arsenal

One Performer. A Full Sound.

My setup is engineered to sound like a full band — because it basically is. Here's what I bring to every show.

Piano & Keys

The foundation of everything. My piano playing anchors the entire performance — from classical ceremony pieces to jazz standards to pop anthems. Classically trained, endlessly versatile.

Vocals

Smooth, expressive, and perfectly calibrated to the moment. My voice is as flexible as my piano — equally at home on a heartfelt ballad or a high-energy crowd favorite.

Guitar

Acoustic and electric. The guitar adds texture, warmth, and range that the keys alone can't provide — perfect for intimate moments or adding a different sonic color to the room.

Live Looping

The secret weapon. Using a professional loop station, I layer live recordings of myself in real time — building full arrangements, beat by beat, entirely live. Audiences are always stunned. It has to be seen to be understood.

Backing Drum Kit

A programmable percussion setup provides the rhythmic backbone that gives every song its full-band feel. Subtle enough for a dinner setting, powerful enough for a reception dance floor.
